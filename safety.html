Our Core Commitment

Fun Without Compromise. Safety in Every Step.

At KidNest, every piece of play equipment, daily cleaning routine, and staff certification exists so families can focus on laughter, connection, and pure joy.

6-Pillar Safety Framework

Built Into Everything We Do

From medical-grade sanitation to dedicated floor supervisors, our safety protocols run 365 days a year.

Hospital-Grade Sanitization

All play equipment, ball pits, and high-touch surfaces undergo systematic non-toxic disinfection between play sessions and deep steaming nightly.

Certified First-Aid Supervisors

Every floor team member is certified in Pediatric First Aid & CPR, actively stationed throughout play zones to guide safe exploration.

Secure Check-in & RFID Bands

Matching parent-child security wristbands ensure no child can leave the facility without their verified parent or guardian.

Age-Segregated Zones

Toddlers (1–3), Juniors (4–7), and Adventurers (8–12) have dedicated play areas so younger kids play gently without older collision hazards.

Impact-Absorbing Soft Surfaces

Engineered multi-layer high-density EVA foam flooring and padded boundary nets prevent falls, bumps, and slip injuries.

HEPA Air Filtration & Climate Control

Continuous fresh air exchange with commercial HEPA H13 filters keeps indoor air clean, dust-free, and allergen-controlled all day.

House Guidelines

Parent & Guest Guidelines

Working together with families is what keeps KidNest safe and enjoyable for every child. Here are a few simple rules for all visitors.

Book a Play Session
Grip Socks Required

Children must wear non-slip grip socks in all play zones. Grip socks are available at reception for ₹60.

Active Parent Supervision

Children under 4 must be accompanied by an adult inside the Toddler Zone at all times.

Designated Snack Areas

Food and drinks are strictly limited to the KidNest Café to keep all play surfaces crumb-free and safe for allergic children.

Stay Home if Unwell

To protect all visitors, any child showing symptoms of fever or cold will be offered a complimentary rebooking for another day.
