Safety & Hygiene

Our 100% Commitment to Safe Play

We maintain the highest safety and cleanliness protocols in the indoor amusement industry so you can relax with total peace of mind.

Certified Child-Safe Facility

Inspected and certified compliant with ASTM & EN-1176 playground standards.

CPR & First-Aid Certified Staff

Every floor monitor is certified in pediatric first aid, emergency response, and vigilant child supervision.

Nightly Hospital-Grade Sanitization

Every surface, ball pit ball, and slide is deep-cleaned nightly using certified eco-friendly UV-C and antimicrobial mists.

Smart RFID Wristband Tracking

Parents and children receive matching secure RFID wristbands. No child can leave the gates without verified parent matching.

High-Density Impact Foam Flooring

Multi-layered shock-absorbing antibacterial padding protects against bumps and slips across all activity zones.
